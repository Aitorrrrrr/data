📈 META ALERTS
Who's getting better or worse on each map, based on your recent battles
How it works: your battle history is split in two — the most recent 30% of battles vs everything before it. Stats are computed per map and across all maps combined ("🌐 All maps combined"), so no map is left out. A brawler appears when its win rate moved ±8 pts (needs 10 recent + 8 older games) or when it's picked ±5 pts more or less often. NEW = it wasn't played enough before to compare, so it just became relevant there.
